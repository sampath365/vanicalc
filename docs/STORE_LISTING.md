## Full description

```
VaniCalc is a fast, private calculator that understands you.

TYPE, SPEAK OR LISTEN
• Full standard keypad with memory, brackets, powers, factorial and percentages
• Scientific mode with trigonometry, logarithms and constants
• Say "25 plus 35" or "15 percent of 200" and hear the answer read back
• Voice commands: clear, delete, repeat, open history, switch to scientific, change language

TEN INDIAN LANGUAGES
English, తెలుగు, हिन्दी, தமிழ், ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം, मराठी, বাংলা, ગુજરાતી, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ.
The interface, number words and speech follow your chosen language. English digits always work.

NUMBER TO WORDS — OFFLINE
Convert any number into words using Indian lakh/crore or international million/billion grouping.
Decimals, negatives and currency are supported:
₹11,800 → "Eleven Thousand Eight Hundred Rupees Only".

MONEY & EVERYDAY TOOLS
• GST calculator with CGST/SGST/IGST breakup and amount in words
• Discount, profit & loss, EMI, simple and compound interest
• Tip split, ratio, fraction, percentage, age and date difference
• Eight-category unit converter

PREMIUM, CALM DESIGN
Light, dark or system theme, five accent colours, large rounded keys, smooth animations and a
responsive layout for phones and tablets. Optional larger text.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no VaniCalc server. History and settings stay on your device.
Speech uses your device's recogniser; text-to-speech uses your device's voice engine.

VaniCalc works offline for calculations, number words and history.
```

## Data safety declaration

- Does the app collect or share any user data? **No.**
- Is data encrypted in transit? Not applicable — the app makes no network requests of its own.
- Can users request data deletion? Yes — in-app history clearing and uninstall.
- Data types: none collected. History and preferences are stored locally only.
- Microphone: used for speech recognition; audio is processed by the device speech service, not
  collected by the app.
