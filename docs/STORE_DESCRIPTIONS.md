## Full description

```
VaniCalc is a fast, private calculator that understands you. Type a sum, speak it naturally, or scan barcodes to count items — with results read aloud in your language.

TYPE, SPEAK OR LISTEN
• Complete standard keypad with memory, brackets, powers, factorial and percentages
• Scientific mode with trigonometry, logarithms and constants
• Live preview and the answer written out in words as you type
• Say “25 plus 35” or “15 percent of 200” and hear the answer
• Voice commands: clear, delete, repeat, open history, switch to scientific, change language
• Speak-on-equals option, plus a speak/stop button on the display

TEN INDIAN LANGUAGES
English, తెలుగు, हिन्दी, தமிழ், ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം, मराठी, বাংলা, ગુજરાતી and ਪੰਜਾਬੀ.
The interface, number words and speech follow your chosen language. English digits always work, and speech falls back gracefully when a device has no voice for a language.

NUMBER TO WORDS — OFFLINE
Convert any number into words with Indian lakh/crore or international million/billion grouping. Decimals, negatives and currency are supported:
₹11,800 → “Eleven Thousand Eight Hundred Rupees Only”.
Copy, share or speak the result in one tap.

SCAN & COUNT
Point the camera at item barcodes to tally stock, purchases or assets. Each scan increments the quantity, prices are optional, and the screen shows total items, total amount, the amount in words and speech output. Items can also be added manually.

MONEY & EVERYDAY TOOLS
• GST calculator with CGST/SGST/IGST breakup and amount in words
• Discount, profit & loss, EMI, simple and compound interest
• Tip split, ratio, fraction, percentage, age and date difference
• Quadratic equation solver, statistics, base converter, GCD/LCM, nPr/nCr and primes
• 19-category unit converter with searchable units

PREMIUM, CALM DESIGN
Light, dark or system theme, five accent colours, large rounded keys, smooth animations, a responsive phone/tablet layout and optional larger text.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no VaniCalc server. History and settings stay on your device. Speech uses your device’s recogniser; text-to-speech uses your device’s voice engine; barcode frames are processed on-device by ML Kit.

VaniCalc works offline for calculations, number words, history and converters. Speech recognition availability depends on your device and language.
```
