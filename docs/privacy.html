VaniCalc Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Published by EGAN Labs

VaniCalc ("the app") is a calculator that works with typed input, speech and barcode scanning. The short version: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no VaniCalc server. Your calculations never leave your device.

Information the app stores

  • Calculation history — expressions, results, timestamps, language and type are stored in a local database (Hive) in the app's private storage.
  • Preferences — theme, accent colour, language, numbering system, speech rate/pitch and auto-speak are stored locally.

This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear it in the app or uninstall the app.

Microphone and speech recognition

Voice input requires microphone permission. When you tap the microphone, audio is sent to the speech recognition service provided by your device or operating system (for example Google's speech service on Android or Apple's speech framework on iOS). That processing is governed by the provider's privacy policy. VaniCalc does not upload, store or transmit your audio or transcripts. Recognised text is converted into a maths expression locally.

Camera and barcode scanning

The scan & count tool uses the camera only while that screen is open. Barcode frames are processed on-device by Google ML Kit and are not stored or transmitted by VaniCalc. Camera access is optional; items can always be entered manually.

Text-to-speech

Results are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The text sent to the engine is generated locally from your calculation.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app hands the selected text to your device's share sheet. You choose the destination; nothing is shared automatically.

Children

VaniCalc is a general-purpose calculator and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

Clear individual entries or the whole history from the History screen, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data. There is no server-side data to delete.
