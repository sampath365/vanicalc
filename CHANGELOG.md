# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-22

### Added
- Camera scan & count: scan barcodes to tally items, set prices, see item and amount
  totals, speak the total and save it to history.
- Advanced maths tools: quadratic equation solver (real and complex roots), statistics
  (mean, median, mode, spread), base converter and number tools (GCD/LCM, nPr/nCr,
  primes and factorisation).
- Searchable dropdowns for language and unit selection.
- Live number words under the calculator display.
- Speak-on-equals when auto-speak is enabled, plus a speak/stop button in the display.

### Changed
- Number-to-words result now sits directly below the input and updates as you type.
- Speak buttons stack cleanly on narrow screens and large text.
- Unit converter expanded to 19 categories (pressure, energy, power, angle, frequency,
  force, torque, acceleration, density, flow, illuminance and more).
- Removed the calculator microphone FAB that could overlap the keypad.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-22

### Added
- Standard and scientific calculator with memory, precedence, percentages, powers,
  roots, factorial, constants and guarded error handling.
- Voice calculator with live recognition, editable interpreted expression, confidence,
  ambiguity confirmation and spoken results.
- Spoken maths in English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali,
  Gujarati and Punjabi, plus voice commands.
- Offline number-to-words engine for ten languages with Indian and international
  grouping, decimals, negatives and currency.
- Text-to-speech for complete calculations, answers and numbers with rate/pitch control.
- Hive-backed searchable history with favourites, reuse, copy, speak and daily statistics.
- GST, discount, profit/loss, EMI, simple/compound interest, tip, ratio, fraction,
  percentage, age, date difference and unit converter tools.
- Premium light/dark themes, five accents, onboarding, splash, language picker and settings.
- 242 unit and widget tests; zero analyzer issues.
