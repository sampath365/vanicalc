# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-22

### Added
- Standard and scientific calculator with memory, precedence, percentages, powers,
  roots, factorial, constants and guarded error handling.
- Voice calculator with live recognition, editable interpreted expression, confidence,
  ambiguity confirmation and spoken results.
- Spoken maths in English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali,
  Gujarati and Punjabi, plus voice commands.
- Offline number-to-words engine for ten languages with Indian and international
  grouping, decimals, negatives and currency.
- Text-to-speech for complete calculations, answers and numbers with rate/pitch control.
- Hive-backed searchable history with favourites, reuse, copy, speak and daily statistics.
- GST, discount, profit/loss, EMI, simple/compound interest, tip, ratio, fraction,
  percentage, age, date difference and unit converter tools.
- Premium light/dark themes, five accents, onboarding, splash, language picker and settings.
- 238 unit and widget tests; zero analyzer issues.
